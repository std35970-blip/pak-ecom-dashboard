## 🌟 Premium Features

### 📊 Enterprise Dashboard
- **KPI Cards**: Revenue, orders, profit with trend indicators (+12.5%, -2.1%)
- **Interactive Charts**: Modern Chart.js visualizations with tooltips
- **Recent Orders Widget**: Quick access to latest transactions
- **Top Products Widget**: Revenue-sorted product performance
- **Quick Summary Panel**: All key metrics at a glance
- **Export Reports**: One-click CSV export

### 📦 Order Management
- **Complete Order CRUD**: Add, view, edit, and delete orders
- **Comprehensive Order Details**:
  - Auto-generated Order IDs
  - Customer Name and Phone
  - City selection (major Pakistani cities)
  - Product details with quantity and pricing
  - Shipping costs
  - Payment methods (COD, JazzCash, Easypaisa, Bank Transfer, Prepaid)
  - Courier selection (TCS, Leopards, PostEx, M&P, Trax, CallCourier, Other)
  - Order status tracking (Pending, Confirmed, Shipped, Delivered, Returned, Cancelled)
- **Smart Filters**: Filter orders by status, payment method, and courier
- **Order History**: Complete order tracking with dates

### 💰 Profit Calculator
- **Comprehensive Cost Analysis**:
  - Product Cost
  - Selling Price
  - Shipping Cost
  - COD Charges (default ₨50)
  - Packaging Cost (default ₨30)
  - Platform Fee percentage
- **Instant Calculations**: Auto-calculate Net Profit and Profit Margin %
- **Visual Feedback**: Color-coded results (green for profit, red for loss)

### 🏦 Payment Tracking
- **Payment Method Analytics**: Track payments via JazzCash, Easypaisa, COD, Bank Transfer, and Prepaid
- **Visual Breakdown**: Pie chart showing payment method distribution
- **Payment Status**: Monitor pending vs received payments
- **Total Revenue by Method**: See which payment methods generate most revenue

### 📈 Product Performance
- **Product Analytics**:
  - Units sold per product
  - Total revenue per product
  - Return rate tracking
- **Smart Sorting**: Identify best-selling and worst-performing products
- **Quick Add**: Easy product addition through order creation

### 🚚 Courier Analytics
- **Courier Distribution**: See which courier handles most orders
- **Performance Metrics**:
  - Return rate per courier
  - Delivery success rate
  - Order volume by courier
- **Visual Comparison**: Doughnut chart for courier distribution

### ⚙️ Settings
- **Business Information**: Store your business name, owner name, phone, and city
- **Default Preferences**: Set default courier and payment method
- **Persistent Storage**: All settings saved locally
